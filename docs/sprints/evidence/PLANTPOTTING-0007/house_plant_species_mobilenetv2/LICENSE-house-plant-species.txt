Dataset-vs-weights note
-----------------------
The model was trained on the Kaggle "House Plant Species" 47-class image set
(https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kacpergregorowicz/house-plant-species), whose
images were community-collected. What is bundled here is the TRAINED MODEL
(weights), redistributed under the repository's Apache-2.0 license — NOT the
training images. This mirrors the standard situation for ImageNet-pretrained
models. Recorded per PLANTPOTTING-0007 Phase 1 (dataset-public ≠ weights-
redistributable) for transparency.
