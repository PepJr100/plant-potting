# Plant Profiles

Fourteen species- and genus-level guides covering the four plant categories the app must handle with the most nuance: aroids (Monstera, Philodendron, Anthurium, Pothos — the dominant houseplant family by volume and the one where chunky substrate matters most), orchids (epiphytes that demand bark, not soil), and carnivorous plants (which require nutrient-free media — adding fertiliser will kill them). Two specialty profiles round out the set: Monstera albo variegata and Philodendron Pink Princess — the highest-value variegated cultivars that bring outsized social-media-driven attention to the entire houseplant market.
