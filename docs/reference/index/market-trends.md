# Market Trends

Nine sources that frame the commercial opportunity behind the app. Three AIPH (International Association of Horticultural Producers) pieces and one StatsMarketResearch report establish the scale and growth trajectory of the global horticulture industry and the UK retail garden centre sector. Five trend-focused articles from The Sill, Living Etc, Happy Houseplants, Love That Leaf, and Terrarium Tribe report what consumers are actually buying in 2025 and what's expected in 2026 — useful both for prioritising which plant species the classifier and substrate engine should support first, and for understanding the buyer's mindset.
