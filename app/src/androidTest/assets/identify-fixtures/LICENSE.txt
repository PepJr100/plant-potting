PLANTPOTTING-0005 §5.4 — instrumentation test fixture.

monstera-deliciosa.jpg
======================

A real photograph of Monstera deliciosa (Swiss cheese plant), sourced from
Wikimedia Commons. It replaces the procedurally-generated synthetic JPEG
(deterministic seed 0x4D4F4E54) used in PLANTPOTTING-0004, so that
OnDeviceModelRealInterpreterTest can make an accuracy-bearing assertion against
a genuine in-vocabulary species (§5.5–§5.6 probe + assertion).

----------

-------------

crassula-ovata.jpg
==================

A real photograph of Crassula ovata (jade plant), sourced from Wikimedia
Commons. PLANTPOTTING-0006 §G1 — the second and final in-vocabulary species in
the AIY V1/3 ↔ KB overlap (the other being Monstera deliciosa), so that
OnDeviceModelRealInterpreterTest can make an accuracy-bearing assertion against
a genuine in-vocabulary species, completing the V0.1 multi-species calibration
sweep (Phase 2 probe + assertion).
