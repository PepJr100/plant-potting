PLANTPOTTING-0005 §5.4 — instrumentation test fixture.

monstera-deliciosa.jpg
======================

A real photograph of Monstera deliciosa (Swiss cheese plant), sourced from
Wikimedia Commons. It replaces the procedurally-generated synthetic JPEG
(deterministic seed 0x4D4F4E54) used in PLANTPOTTING-0004, so that
OnDeviceModelRealInterpreterTest can make an accuracy-bearing assertion against
a genuine in-vocabulary species (§5.5–§5.6 probe + assertion).

Provenance
----------

- Title:        HK SW Leaves with holes.JPG
- Depicts:      Monstera deliciosa (leaves with characteristic fenestration),
                Hong Kong
- Source page:  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HK_SW_Leaves_with_holes.JPG
- Original file: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4b/HK_SW_Leaves_with_holes.JPG
- Author:       Princesleaf (Wikimedia Commons)
- Date taken:   29 September 2008
- Retrieved:    4 June 2026

Modifications
-------------

The original (1600×1200, ~1.17 MB) was scaled and centre-cropped to 480×480 and
re-encoded as JPEG quality 80 (~41 KB) for use as a compact test fixture. No
other alterations.

crassula-ovata.jpg
==================

A real photograph of Crassula ovata (jade plant), sourced from Wikimedia
Commons. PLANTPOTTING-0006 §G1 — the second and final in-vocabulary species in
the AIY V1/3 ↔ KB overlap (the other being Monstera deliciosa), so that
OnDeviceModelRealInterpreterTest can make an accuracy-bearing assertion against
a genuine in-vocabulary species, completing the V0.1 multi-species calibration
sweep (Phase 2 probe + assertion).

Provenance
----------

- Title:        Jade Plant, Crassula ovata IMG 3632
- Depicts:      Crassula ovata (potted jade plant, top-down view of fleshy
                oval red-edged leaves)
- Source page:  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jade_Plant,_Crassula_ovata_IMG_3632.jpg
- Original file: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/84/Jade_Plant%2C_Crassula_ovata_IMG_3632.jpg
- Author:       S.G.S. (Wikimedia Commons)
- Date taken:   17 May 2018
- Retrieved:    4 June 2026

Modifications
-------------

The original (6000×4000, "own work") was centre-cropped to a square and scaled
to 480×480 and re-encoded as JPEG quality 80 (~30 KB) for use as a compact test
